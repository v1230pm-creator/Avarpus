---
name: Public legal publication gate
description: Conditions that must be met before the official site's data-collecting public forms and legal notices are published.
---

Do not describe the official site as legally publish-ready while the operator/controller identity, usable privacy contact, and reviewed effective notice date remain unverified. Pre-release notices may describe product behavior, but they are not substitutes for an operator-specific privacy notice and service terms.

**Why:** The public support and account-deletion forms collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, and request content. Publishing those forms with placeholder legal/contact details creates avoidable privacy and trust risk.

**How to apply:** Before public production release, replace pre-release legal wording with reviewed operator-specific notices, a verified privacy contact, a fixed effective date, and reviewed retention disclosures. Never invent these details to unblock publication.
