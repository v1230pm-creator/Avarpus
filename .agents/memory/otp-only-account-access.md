---
name: OTP-only account access
description: Product policy for primary sign-in and retired verification data.
---

Mobile OTP is the sole primary account sign-in method across mobile and web. Saved email addresses remain read-only recovery and backup identities; they must not become a competing primary sign-in path.

**Why:** The account model must retain existing users and historic business data while eliminating SIM-originated SMS verification and device-key authentication complexity.

**How to apply:** Send request and resend OTPs to every valid normalized Indian mobile number, regardless of whether an active account already exists. OTP verification may issue a session only when that number maps to an eligible active account; it must never auto-create an account or shop. Registration and customer-invite activation may provision or link an account, but must return no token and route people to mobile OTP before access. Keep the established OTP provider, expiry, rate limiting, and one-time verification controls. Do not reintroduce SIM verification, SMS bridge callbacks, device-key registration, native keystore login, or SMS-send permissions. Keep historical database tables and applied migrations intact unless a separately approved, non-destructive data-retention plan replaces them.
