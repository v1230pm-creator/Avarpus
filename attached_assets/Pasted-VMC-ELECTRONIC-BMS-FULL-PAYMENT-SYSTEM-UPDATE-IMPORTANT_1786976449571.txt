1. CORE PAYMENT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Separate these two concepts completely:

A. REPAIR ESTIMATE
B. FINAL BILL

The estimate entered when receiving a phone MUST NOT automatically become the final bill.

Payment flow must be:

RECEIVED
→ ESTIMATE
→ OPTIONAL ADVANCE
→ REPAIR
→ FINAL BILL
→ PAYMENT
→ PARTIAL / PAID / DUE
→ LATER PAYMENT
→ POSSIBLE REFUND / ADJUSTMENT

All steps must remain synchronized with the existing repair timeline.

==================================================

Show:

Optional Advance Payment

Advance Paid: ₹500
Estimated Balance: ₹700

IMPORTANT:

The estimated amount is NOT the final bill.

Staff/dealer/technician must not be forced to collect the estimated amount.
