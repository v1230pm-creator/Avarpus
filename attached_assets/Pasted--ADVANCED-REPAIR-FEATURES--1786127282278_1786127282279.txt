=====================================
ADVANCED REPAIR FEATURES
=====================================

Build a complete enterprise-grade repair workflow.

1. PHOTO & DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT
- Capture device photos during receiving.
- Front, Back, Left, Right, Top, Bottom.
- Capture damaged area photos.

- Upload purchase bill.
- Upload customer ID (optional).
- Store all images securely with Repair ID.

2. CUSTOMER SIGNATURE

- Digital signature while delivery.
- Save signatures permanently.
